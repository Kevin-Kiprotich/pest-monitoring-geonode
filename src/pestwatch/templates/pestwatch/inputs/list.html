The weather and the seasonal forecast are downloaded automatically. The monthly satellite composites are made in Google Earth Engine every month (Processes > Satellite composites), with the modelling team's recipes; they can also be uploaded here, e.g. the team's own history or a month Earth Engine could not make. An uploaded file is never replaced by the automatic fetch. The other datasets are uploaded by a manager. Each file is checked and stored where the models read it. Larger archives (e.g. years of past composites) can also be copied into {{ data_root }}/data on the server in the same folder layout; those are kept as well.
